## Getting started

Add new posts by creating files in the `_posts/` directory with the naming convention `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` and the following front matter:

```yaml
---
title: Your Post Title
date: 2026-04-12
---
```

Then write your content in Markdown below the front matter.
